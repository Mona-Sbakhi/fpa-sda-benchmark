Highlights
- Plant-inspired FPA-SDA hybrid benchmarked under equal budgets and 50 paired runs
- The hybrid does not outperform SDA and trails GA, PSO, DE and L-SHADE
- Lengthening the FPA phase degrades the hybrid; SDA alone is as good as any schedule
- A redesigned hybrid matches L-SHADE, but only through differential-evolution parts
- Code, configurations and per-run predictions are released for full reproducibility
